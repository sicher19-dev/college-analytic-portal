# College Analytic Portal (sample)

A sample of the weekly dashboard No Brand Baseball Analytics builds for college and university programs: hitters, pitchers and the transfer portal in one place.

**Every player, team and number on this site is fictional.** It shows what a program gets, not anyone's real data.

## What's in it

- **Home:** the week at a glance. Hitting and pitching side by side, who to watch, this weekend's opponent, the bullpen for Tuesday and the transfer portal board.
- **Hitters:** the coaches' page for the lineup, plus a Monday card for every hitter. Each card shows his numbers, contact point against 90+ mph, balls in play, and his development plan with cage numbers next to game numbers.
- **Pitchers:** the staff page and a Monday card for every arm: velo and pitch shape by week, arsenal, development plan with bullpen numbers next to game numbers, and who is available Tuesday.
- **Opponent:** their starters for your hitters, their lineup for your pitchers, and which of your arms match their starter for live BP.
- **Practice:** each hitter's Wednesday session and each pitcher's Wednesday bullpen, scored against his own targets, plus monthly test days.
- **Transfer portal:** plug a transfer into your roster and answer four questions. Does he compete at your level? Would he play for you? Does he make you better? Is he worth spending big on?
- **Program:** what the package includes.

Every view has its own link (for example `#/pitcher/mason-lyle` or `#/portal/rowan-pike`), so you can send a player straight to his card. Cards and evaluations also print.

## How it works

The site is one self-contained `index.html` with the data built in. It needs no build step and no server code.
