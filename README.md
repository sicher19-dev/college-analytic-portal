# College Hitting Dashboard (sample)

A sample of the weekly hitting dashboard No Brand Baseball Analytics builds for college and university programs.

**Every player, team and number on this site is fictional.** It shows what a program gets, not anyone's real data.

## What's in it

- **Team:** the coaches' page. It has every hitter's numbers, this week's flags and the team against each pitch type.
- **Hitters:** a Monday card for every hitter. Each card shows his numbers, contact point against 90+ mph, balls in play, his development plan with cage numbers next to game numbers, and one thing to work on.
- **Opponent:** the weekend starter's arsenal, mix by count, what to hunt and what to take, and which pitchers on your staff match him for live BP.
- **Practice:** each hitter's Wednesday session scored against his own targets, plus his monthly test days.
- **Roster:** a portal read on a transfer bat and a draft read on a junior.
- **Program:** what the package includes.

Every view has its own link (for example `#/hitter/danny-ortega`), so you can send a hitter straight to his card. Cards also print.

## How it works

The site is one self-contained `index.html` with the data built in. It needs no build step and no server code.
